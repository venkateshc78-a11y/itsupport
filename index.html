Frequently Asked Questions

Search below or browse common questions about IT support.

How do I reset my network password?
Go to the corporate self-service portal and select "Reset Password," then verify your identity using your registered mobile number or email. If you're locked out of the portal itself, call the Service Desk Hotline and a technician will verify your identity and reset it manually. Passwords must be changed within 15 minutes of a reset for security reasons.
How long will my ticket take to be resolved?
Resolution time depends on the priority you select when submitting the ticket: Critical issues target 1 hour, High priority targets 4 hours, Medium priority targets 1 business day, and Low priority targets 3 business days. You'll receive updates as your ticket progresses through the queue.
How do I connect to the corporate VPN from home?
Install the approved VPN client from the software portal on your company laptop, then log in using your corporate credentials and the multi-factor authentication code from your registered device. If the client won't connect, check that you're on a stable internet connection and that your laptop's operating system is up to date. Persistent connection issues should be logged as a "Network & VPN" ticket.
My laptop won't boot — what should I do?
First, hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a shutdown, then try powering on again after 30 seconds. If it still won't boot, check that the charger is connected and the battery indicator light is on. If the problem persists, submit a "Hardware" ticket with Priority set to at least High, since a non-booting laptop affects your ability to work.
How do I request new software to be installed?
Submit a ticket under the "Software / Application" category with the name, version, and business justification for the software you need. Most standard, pre-approved applications are installed within 1 business day, while non-standard software may require manager and security approval before installation. You'll be notified by email once the installation is complete.
I received a suspicious email — what now?
Do not click any links or open attachments in the email. Use the "Report Phishing" button in your email client if available, or forward the email as an attachment to the security team and submit a "Security Incident" ticket describing what you saw. Never reply with any credentials, OTPs, or personal information, even if the email appears to be from IT or a colleague.
How do I get access to a shared drive or application?
Submit a ticket under "Account & Access," specifying the exact drive, folder, or application name and your business reason for needing it. Access requests typically require approval from your department head before they can be granted. Once approved, access is usually provisioned within 1 business day.
How do I track or update an existing ticket?
You'll receive your ticket reference number (formatted like UOB-ITSD-YYYYMMDD-####) immediately after submission by email. Reply to that confirmation email at any time to add updates or additional information, or call the Service Desk Hotline and quote your reference number. In this training demo, ticket data is only kept in memory for the current browser session and is not actually tracked anywhere.
